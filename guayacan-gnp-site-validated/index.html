Why Guayacan

One of Colombia's quietest birding corners.

20 minutes from Calarcá, 45 from Armenia — inside the Coffee Cultural Landscape, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Colombia holds more bird species than any other country on Earth, and this pocket of the Central Andes foothills is where that diversity concentrates.

The Preserve

A private reserve, not a park.

Guayacan is a small, family-protected natural forest in the Quebrada Negra area — native trees over fifty years old, a single trail, and enough quiet that birds behave like they own the place, because they do.

  • One reservation at a time by session — no crowds, no competing tripods.
  • Two water sources draw activity down to eye level in the early morning.
  • Seasonal hiking and year-round hummingbird and moth activity in the garden edge.

For the Photographer

Built for patience, not crowds.

Every visit is a single private session — your group, your pace, no one else's flash going off behind you.

Golden-hour access

Morning sessions are timed to the light Colombian cloud forests are known for — soft, low, and directional through the canopy edge.

One group at a time

No shared sessions or competing schedules — the trail, the light, and the birds are yours for the visit.

Macro & night subjects too

The forest edge draws moths and butterflies alongside the birds — bring a macro lens, not just glass for the canopy.
